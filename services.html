Service 02

Remediation Guidance & Support

An audit without a clear remediation path isn't useful. Every finding we deliver includes prioritized, actionable guidance your team can act on immediately.

Prioritized Findings Reports

Issues ranked by severity — critical down to minor. Your team knows exactly what to fix first and why it matters most. No guessing about priority.

Compliant Code Snippet Recommendations

Reports include specific, ready-to-reference HTML and ARIA code snippets that demonstrate the correct implementation. Your developers see exactly what the fix looks like — not just what standard was violated.

Follow-Up Verification Testing

After your team implements fixes, we come back and test. We confirm each resolved item actually works correctly with a screen reader — not just that the code changed. Included in Professional and Enterprise audits; available as a standalone service.

Service 04

Accessibility Consulting

Expert advisory across the accessibility compliance spectrum — from ADA Title II strategy to ADA Title II, Section 508, and WCAG 2.2 standards. Quint speaks from 25+ years of lived screen reader expertise, not boilerplate compliance checklists.

ADA Title II Compliance Strategy

California ADA Title II deadlines are approaching and enforcement is real. We help organizations understand their obligations, assess current state, and build a remediation roadmap that gets them compliant before the deadline — not after an enforcement action.

ADA Title II — State & Local Government

ADA Title II requires state and local government entities to make their digital services accessible. We help agencies assess compliance gaps, document remediation efforts, and prepare for DOJ scrutiny.

Section 508 Compliance

Federal agencies and their contractors must meet Section 508 standards. We provide gap analysis, VPAT preparation support, and testing that holds up under procurement review.

WCAG 2.2 Expert Advisory

Direct advisory on WCAG 2.2 success criteria interpretation, testing methodologies, and how standards apply to your specific implementation patterns. We don't recite the spec — we explain how it plays out in practice with real assistive technology.

Expert advisory on ARIA patterns, keyboard navigation, and WCAG 2.2 conformance. We work through complex accessibility problems with your team directly — developers, QA, and product owners.
