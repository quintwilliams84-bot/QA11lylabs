6. Scan Limitations and Disclaimer

Important: Automated accessibility scans detect approximately 30–40% of WCAG 2.2 accessibility issues. Automated scanning is not a substitute for manual accessibility testing and expert review.

Our automated scan tool, and the automated component of our paid audits, use rule-based testing against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at the AA conformance level. The following limitations apply:

  • Incomplete coverage — automated tools can detect certain classes of accessibility issues (such as missing alt text, color contrast failures, and missing form labels) but cannot evaluate all aspects of accessibility, including many cognitive, motor, and contextual requirements.
  • No guarantee of conformance — a passing automated scan does not certify, warrant, or guarantee that your website is fully WCAG 2.2 AA conformant or legally compliant with any accessibility law (including the ADA, Section 508, or EN 301 549).
  • Context-dependent issues — many accessibility issues require human judgment to evaluate, such as the quality and appropriateness of alternative text, the logical reading order of content, and the usability of interactive components.
  • Dynamic content — single-page applications, content loaded via JavaScript, and interactive widgets may not be fully evaluated in a standard automated scan.

For comprehensive accessibility assurance, we recommend combining automated scanning with a full manual audit performed by our specialists. Contact us at qa11ylabs.com/contact.html to discuss a full audit engagement.

Scan results reflect the state of the submitted URL at the time of scanning. We make no representation that results will remain accurate if the website's content or code changes.
