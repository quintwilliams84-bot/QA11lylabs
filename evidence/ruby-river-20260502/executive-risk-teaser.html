Why it matters
If a button has no programmatic name, screen reader users may not know what action they are about to take. On revenue pages, that can block ordering, booking, checkout, or lead submission.
Risk framing
Accessibility issues can increase exposure under WCAG-based procurement requirements, Section 508 expectations, state accessibility laws, and ADA-related demand letters. This teaser is not legal advice; it is a practical risk signal that should be validated through a full audit.
