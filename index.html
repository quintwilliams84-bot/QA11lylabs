Why QA11Y Labs

We don't test accessibility.
We live it.

Most accessibility consultants run automated tools and hand you a spreadsheet. We bring something no tool can replicate — 25+ years as a daily screen reader user. We experience the web the way your disabled users do, which means we find barriers that automated scanners miss entirely.

Lighthouse scored the module we audited for SSLC at 100/100 for accessibility. Our manual tree inspection found 69 critical violations that Lighthouse never touched.

  • Real screen reader testing NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver — validated on actual assistive technology, not simulations.
  • Automated + manual, combined axe-core, Playwright accessibility tree, and Lighthouse — then we go further by hand.
  • Code-level remediation reports Every finding comes with the exact HTML/ARIA fix — not just a reference to the WCAG criterion.
  • Legal defensibility Structured VPAT-ready documentation that holds up under ADA Title II and Section 508 scrutiny.
